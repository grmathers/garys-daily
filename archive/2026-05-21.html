OpenAI's Reasoning Model Disproves an 80-Year-Old Erdős Conjecture in Discrete Geometry

An internal OpenAI reasoning model independently disproved the planar unit distance conjecture posed by Paul Erdős in 1946, finding an infinite family of point arrangements that beats the square grid by a polynomial factor. For nearly 80 years, mathematicians assumed grids were essentially optimal; the model published a verifiable proof, and several respected mathematicians independently confirmed the result. Crucially, this came from a general-purpose reasoning model rather than a math-specialized system — the first time a prominent open problem central to a mathematical subfield has been solved autonomously by AI. A meaningful inflection point for the "AI as collaborator in real research" thesis.
