Harvey / TLDR AI

vLLM V0 to V1: Correctness Before Corrections in RL

A deep technical post on the vLLM V1 update, which addressed a series of subtle correctness gaps in inference: logprob computation, runtime defaults, in-flight weight updates, and final projection precision. Fixes included adjusting processed logprobs, disabling prefix caching, matching weight update models, and forcing fp32 lm_head computation to align with V0 behavior. The post is a useful case study in why the next phase of inference engineering is about precision parity rather than raw throughput.

NVIDIA Spectrum-X Adds MRC for Gigascale AI Training Fabrics

NVIDIA's open AI-native ethernet fabric Spectrum-X now supports Multipath Reliable Connection. A single RDMA connection can distribute traffic across multiple network paths, lifting GPU utilization by load-balancing across all available paths and giving operators fine-grained visibility for faster troubleshooting at scale. With OpenAI also open-sourcing the protocol with AMD, Intel, Microsoft, and Broadcom this week, MRC is shaping up as the new standard for very large training runs.
