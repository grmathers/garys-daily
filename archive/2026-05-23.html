#3

OpenAI's Reasoning Model Disproves an 80 Year Old Erdős Conjecture, and Mathematicians Verify the Proof

OpenAI announced its reasoning model autonomously disproved Paul Erdős's 1946 planar unit distance conjecture in discrete geometry, finding a new family of point configurations that outperform the previously accepted bound. The company calls it the first time AI has autonomously solved a prominent open problem central to a field of mathematics, and unlike a similar claim seven months ago that was later retracted, this one has been independently verified by leading mathematicians before the announcement. The unlock is the model's ability to maintain long chains of reasoning and stitch ideas across number theory, combinatorics, and geometry. The lab claims the same long horizon reasoning capability will extend to biology, physics, engineering, and medicine, which is the same framing Jack Clark used at Oxford this week when he forecast an AI assisted Nobel discovery inside 12 months.

Microsoft Open Sources RAMPART and Clarity to Bake Agent Safety Into the Dev Workflow

Microsoft released two new MIT licensed tools to operationalize agent safety inside normal engineering workflows. RAMPART is an agent test framework built on PyRIT, designed to encode adversarial and benign scenarios as repeatable CI tests, with deep coverage of cross prompt injection attacks. Clarity is a specification and validation framework that lets teams formally describe product assumptions and safety requirements before writing agent code, then continuously verifies the agent adheres to those specs. Both ship today on GitHub and slot into existing CI pipelines.
