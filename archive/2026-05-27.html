Pope Leo XIV Releases His First Encyclical, "Magnifica Humanitas," and Calls for AI to Be "Disarmed"

Pope Leo XIV published his first encyclical on May 25, "Magnifica Humanitas" ("Magnificent Humanity"), naming AI specifically and arguing that the technology must be freed from "armed" logic of geopolitical and commercial competition. The document declares that "it is not permissible to entrust lethal or otherwise irreversible decisions to artificial systems," directly rebuts the "just war" tradition in the context of AI weapons, and calls for monopolistic control of AI to be opened to public debate. Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, NCR and Time all covered the manifesto, and it lands as a counterweight to the Trump administration's tech first AI posture.

Demis Hassabis: AGI Lands by 2030, Plus or Minus a Year — and We Already Know Which Gaps Are Left

In an exclusive Rundown AI interview taped at I/O and released today, Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis said AGI is on track for 2030, give or take a year, with the unsolved gaps being world physics, memory, consistency, and continual learning. Hassabis says drug discovery timelines have hardened with oncology and immunology first, eventually expanding into "an engine that could help cure any disease." Pair with Jensen Huang's same day comments rejecting the "AI proof career" framing, and the headline message is consistent: the timeline is short, the gaps are specific, and the human edge is real but narrower than people hope.

Stanford: 4M Job Applications, 156 Employers, and Clear Racial Bias in Shared AI Hiring Models

Stanford released a study of 4M job applications across 156 employers using Pymetrics's AI hiring system, finding 10.62% of positions show adverse impact against Black applicants and 5.32% against Asians, with 4% of applicants who applied to 10 positions rejected from all of them — a higher rejection rate than independent decisions would produce. The compounding mechanism is the unlock: 42 models are shared across employers, so a single biased model rejects a candidate at every company using it. The data window is 2018 to 2022, but the architectural lesson (shared infra silently compounds bias) is the durable bit.
