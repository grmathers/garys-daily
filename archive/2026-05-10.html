Eli Lilly Brings LillyPod Online: Pharma's Largest AI Supercomputer Goes Into Production

Eli Lilly switched on LillyPod, the most powerful AI supercomputer in the pharmaceutical industry, built on an NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with 1,016 Blackwell Ultra GPUs and delivering more than 9,000 petaflops of AI performance.
