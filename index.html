California Signs a Pioneering AI Workforce Executive Order the Day After Meta's 8,000 Layoffs

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed an executive order, the first of its kind in any state, directing agencies to prepare workers, small businesses, and communities for AI workforce disruption. Can Sparse Autoencoders Actually Capture Neural Geometry?

A 6 minute research summary covers how sparse autoencoders can be used to study the curved manifolds inside transformer activations, with three distinct strategies (shattering, compact capture, dilution) for representing them. The headline finding: each SAE feature only captures part of the full manifold, so clustering features is essential to recover global structure. An unsupervised pipeline along these lines could meaningfully improve mechanistic interpretability.
